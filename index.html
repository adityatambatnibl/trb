Sources. Geometry from NRB sheets DS 45 / CH-337 and DS 44 / CH-336 & CH-339. Ratings per ISO 76 (C0r) and ISO 281 (Cr, bm = 1.1, fc interpolated from the radial-roller table). Boundary data: 481 rows read from TRB ISO SCAN EXCEL.xlsx, series 2–7.

Method. The apex radius is recovered exactly from the table: DS 45 gives G = 2R·sinα, and G is the outer raceway large-end diameter E — so R = E / (2 sinα). Only the roller taper angle γ is left open, and that is what kd sets. Values tagged EST depend on kd, kL, CR or P; values tagged ISO come straight from the table or from α alone.
